About Flyback Cobalt

An independent guide for anyone who enjoys watches that do more than tell the time, especially when they come in a beautiful shade of blue.

Our story

It started with a pusher

Flyback Cobalt began with a simple question: why do some chronographs need three presses to restart while others need only one? The answer led into a world of column wheels, clutches, reset hammers and aviation history.

Along the way, we became fascinated by blue dials and the surprising number of ways to create them. This website shares what we learned in clear, practical language.

Independent

We do not sell, value or authenticate watches, and we are not affiliated with any watch brand or retailer.

Hands-on

Our interactive tools let you see how chronographs and their scales work, not just read about them.

Accurate

We check technical details against horological references and update pages when we learn more.

How we work

Research and real watchmakers

Our guides draw on horological books, technical documentation, conversations with watchmakers and hands-on experience. We explain how mechanisms generally work rather than reviewing or ranking products.
