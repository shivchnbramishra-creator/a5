Editorial Policy

The standards behind every Flyback Cobalt guide.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Readers rely on Flyback Cobalt for clear, accurate information. These standards guide everything we publish.

Independence

We do not accept payment, free products or gifts in exchange for coverage, and our guides are brand-neutral. Advertisers, if present, have no influence over our content.

Research

We draw on horological references, technical documentation and conversations with watchmakers. Technical facts and historical dates are checked against recognised sources.

Responsible writing

  • We describe how mechanisms generally work rather than reviewing or ranking products.
  • We never make claims about the value, resale prices or authenticity of specific watches.
  • We explain technical terms in plain language.
  • We note where designs and details vary between makers.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please tell us through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed mistakes promptly and update the page date for significant changes.

Images and tools

Photographs are sourced from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and described with accurate alternative text. Interactive tools are created by us and offer general guidance only.
